Privacy Policy

Nexora Player

Effective date: November 24, 2026  ·  Developer: Nexora Labs

01 Overview

Nexora Player ("the App") is developed and published by Nexora Labs ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

02 Information we collect

Device and security data. To protect the App and its users from tampered or counterfeit copies, we read a non-reversible hash derived from the App's own installation signing certificate and basic device/app version information. This is used only for integrity verification and is not linked to your identity.

Advertising data. The App displays ads through third-party advertising networks, which may collect your advertising identifier and related technical data to serve and measure ads. See section 4 for the networks we use.

Your playlists. If you add an Xtream Codes or M3U playlist, those credentials and URLs are stored locally on your device so the App can connect to the service you chose. We do not collect, store, or have access to this information on our own servers.

Crash and diagnostic data. Basic crash reports and performance data are collected to help us fix bugs and improve stability.

We do not require account registration to use the App, and we do not collect your name, email address, or payment details directly within the App.

03 How we use this information

  • Operate, maintain, and secure the App, including detecting unauthorized or modified copies.
  • Display and personalize advertising, and measure ad performance.
  • Diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and improve performance.
  • Enforce fair and legitimate use of the App.

04 Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob & Google Play Services
  • AppLovin MAX
  • Unity Ads
  • ironSource
  • StartApp
  • Firebase (Analytics, Remote Config)

These providers may independently collect data such as advertising identifiers, IP address, and device information under their own privacy practices.

05 Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Information is shared only with the service providers listed above, strictly to operate advertising and core app functionality on our behalf.

06 Your choices

  • You can opt out of personalized ads from your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization, or Reset advertising ID).
  • Uninstalling the App removes all playlists and settings stored locally on your device.

07 Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

08 Data security & retention

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by the App. Playlist data remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the App.

09 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted here, with the effective date updated accordingly. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
